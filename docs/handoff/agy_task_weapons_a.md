One file per weapon in `src/content/weapons/`. Stats, params and tuning go in `content.json`: rare weapons 22-42 base damage, legendary 38-60 with `ability.charges: 1`. Lightning uses the radiant element. Names stay as the catalogue names. Designs are inspired by the film originals in silhouette, proportions, colour and signature motion, but never use trademarked marks (no star on the shield, no knot etching on the hammer). All sound is synthesised in WebAudio (`audio/sfx.ts`): add `thunder()` (noise burst, low-pass 90-400 Hz with a long decay, sub boom, high crack), `quake()`, `ping()` (metal ricochet), `thwip()`, `repulsor()`, `twang()`.

For each weapon provide: a new 16x16 icon, a distinct in-flight sprite with spin and trail, a launch description line, a tap description line (shown on cards), the button ability label, the effect with visuals and sound, and a deterministic seeded-RNG implementation (use `ctx.rng`). Particle effects go through the existing fx kit within the particle cap (90).

1. **Skyforge Maul** (legendary, source: L12 Iron Warlord). Thrown spinning like a hammer (square grey-silver head, short strapped handle). Tap: calls a lightning bolt from the top of the screen onto the nearest monster (jagged pixel bolt, white screen flash, 3-frame hit-stop), then leaves a crackling blue-white electric field around that spot for 3 s (180 steps) that chains damage (a small tick every 20 steps) between monsters and metal blocks within radius. Thunder sound and shake. The Maul can be recalled: a second tap returns it, hitting things on the way.
2. **Comet Fists** (legendary, source: L4 Goblin Chief). Two huge green-grey gauntlet fists thrown together. Tap: dive-slam straight down. On impact an earthquake: expanding ground rings, blocks within +-90 px along the ground hop and take damage, monsters in the wave are knocked flat and stunned 0.6 s, dust wave and rubble particles, low rumble and thud. It does not launch debris into the sky.
3. **Bulwark Disc** (legendary, source: L8 Dark Elf Matron). A round concentric-ring shield in Mash32 tones with a different emblem (a crown or rune, no star). It ricochets off blocks, ground and terrain walls up to 6 times. Each bounce damages what it touches at +10% per bounce, with a spark and a ping. It keeps flying until it leaves the frame (the world edge), then is gone. Tap: redirects once toward the nearest monster.
4. **Steersong Arrow** (rare, source: L3 chest). A red-fletched arrow with an explosive tip, flying point-first. Tap: arms the explosion (the default), detonating with a small bomb blast of radius ~38 (flame, smoke puff). A second tap before impact swaps to a split-tip: three arrows fan out at +-8 degrees. Twang on launch.
5. **Silkline Shooters** (rare, source: L5 chest). Wrist web-shooters. Tap: fires a web line to the nearest monster or block (a white pixel web strand drawn from the shot to the target, a splat on hit). The target is pulled 16 px toward the sling, and webbed monsters are slowed 50% for 4 s. Thwip sound.
6. **Kinetic Palms** (rare, source: L7 chest). Glowing cyan palm repulsors. Tap: a three-shot repulsor volley (fast flat bolts, 10 steps apart), each knocking back what it hits, with a small recoil on the shot. Repulsor pew x3.

## APPROVED v2 ART SPEC (overrides any smaller icon sizes above)
The owner approved the v2 designs. Match them: read `docs/handoff/relic_art_reference.js` (procedural generators for all six weapons, approved look) and look at the three reference images in `docs/handoff/ref/` (`ref_hammer_sheet.webp` = animation and FX sheet structure, `ref_fist.png` = fist shading and finger segmentation, `ref_reactor.png` = the reactor-style core of the Kinetic Palms). The references are INSPIRATION for detail, shading and sheet structure: never copy their pixels into the game, redraw in our palette.
- Hero sprites are 32x32 (not 16x16), 3-4 tone shading with a highlight, auto-outline (reuse the Px kit outline), light from the top-left. Shop/Loadout card icons are the hero sprite downscaled cleanly (or a dedicated 24x24 redraw) with no mushy edges.
- Each weapon ships a frame set: idle/hero, tilt (in flight), mid-spin with motion arc, charged (aura), and for the Maul a planted-with-impact frame; plus effect sprites: Maul swoosh, speed lines, bolt, ground burst; Fists quake rings and dust wave; Disc trail ghosts, ricochet spark and redirect aura; Arrow armed glow, blast and split tips; Shooters strand and splat; Palms charging ring, three-bolt volley and standalone core emblem.
- Rotated and spin frames: pre-render at 15 degree steps with nearest-neighbour from the hero sprite, or draw procedurally, so there is no blur; cache as textures at boot.
- Signature details that must read at game scale (zoom 0.6-1.0): the Maul's squared head, hatched end caps, engraved knot plate and rope-wrapped handle with a loop; the Fist's four curled fingers with crease lines and thumb across; the Disc's concentric rings with a crown emblem (no star); the Arrow's red fletchings and glowing explosive bulb; the Shooters' bracer with straps, nozzle and web spokes; the Palms' armoured palm with the cyan segmented core and white triangle.
- Keep each emblem (knot, crown, reactor core) as a separate layer or function so it can be swapped later with a one-line change.
- Lightning, quake rings, ricochet sparks etc. are drawn through the fx kit within the particle cap, with seeded RNG (`ctx.rng`) for any gameplay-affecting randomness; cosmetic randomness may use the fx kit as usual.
- Review your own screenshots against the references at 2x zoom before finishing; redo any sprite that looks muddy or generic. Put a contact sheet of all frames and effects in `docs/handoff/shots/weapons/sheet.png`.
