# Task x6a: realm 5 "Ember Forge" (levels 17-20) and the Omnitrix Dial

Follow `docs/handoff/AGY_BRIEF.md` and `architecture/RECIPES.md`. Log entry first. Design source: `docs/monster_mash/09_expansion_design.md` sections 4 and 5. Pattern to copy: realm 4 (see the LOG entry "X3 realm 4 Frost Peaks built directly"): realm row in `content.json`, `config.json` levels list, background style in `art/backgrounds.ts`, `LIGHT` entry in `MapScene` (the Map now has pages of 3 realms: realm 4 and 5 share page 2, realm 6 comes next), monsters with a `sprite` field, `MONSTER_ART` entries, levels in `scripts/build_levels.py`, bands in `balance_bands.json`, `tools/regress.mjs` level lists. `regress:quick` is the loop, the full `regress` once at the end.

Art is already generated and processed by Claude with the RUN CLI (do NOT call `rundot generate`): `game/public/sprites/monsters/{lava_imp,forge_ogre,forge_tyrant}.png` and `game/public/sprites/relics/w_omnitrix_dial(.png,_card.png)`. If a processed file is missing, run `powershell -NoProfile -File tools/process_sprites.ps1` (add the ids to its maps first; sources are in `docs/handoff/shots/gen/`).

1. **Realm 5** `ember_forge`, levels 17-20, background `ember` (red furnace caves, lava glow from below, ember motes, dark basalt terrain texture, fits the existing 'keep' look but hotter and darker, clearly different from Iron Keep).
2. **Monsters**: `lava_imp` (flying like `imp`, fire: burns blocks it touches, HP 25), `forge_ogre` (HP 260, armour 10, swings a hammer visual every 5 s), boss `forge_tyrant` (HP 1100, armour 18, `boss: true`; behaviour: every 6 s the lava rises (visual and a damage tick on monsters standing low), an anvil weak point on its back opens for 2 s every 7 s for 2x). Behaviour files in `src/content/monsters/`, numbers in data.
3. **Hazards**: lava gaps in the terrain (a polyline dip with a lava texture; any monster that falls in dies, giving score). Add terrain `hazard: 'lava'` in level data and schema.
4. **Levels 17-20** (`shots: 6`, ramp 17 < 18 < 19 < 20 boss, a recommended `loadout` per level using commons so the solver does not use legendaries, reach respected, 3-5 solver shots, no one-shot wins, nothing unreachable, score targets about 70% of the solver score). Level 19 has a hidden treasure vault chest (`chests`) with reward `w_omnitrix_dial` reachable but off the greedy path; the boss drops no weapon (the Omnitrix is the chest prize).
5. **Omnitrix Dial** (legendary, `w_omnitrix_dial`, unlock `{ chest: "<id>", level: 19 }`, base damage 46, `ability.charges: 1`): the tap flashes green (hourglass sparks at the dial), then FIVE giant forearms fall from the sky across a 150 px band centred on the nearest monster. Each arm is a different alien arm and effect: red fire claw (burn status), green brute fist (knocks flat 0.8 s), cyan crystal arm (extra damage to crystal and iron), grey stone arm (heavy damage x1.6), yellow lightning arm (stun 1 s). Each arm hits, bounces once off the ground or a block (second hit at 60%), then retracts back up into the sky. Draw the arms with the fx kit (pixel forearm silhouettes in the five colours, motion streaks, impact rings), arms fall staggered 6 steps apart. Synthesized `sfx.omnitrix()`: dial click, rising whine, five staggered rubbery thuds, whoosh as they leave. One file in `src/content/weapons/`, registry entry, content row, schema, card text.
6. **Tests and screenshots**: assertions for the Omnitrix (five arms spawn, each hits twice, each retracts, charge used, chest grants it once), the forge tyrant weak point, lava death, levels 17-20 bands, Map page 2 holding realms 4 and 5. Real peak-frame screenshots into `docs/handoff/shots/realm5/`, reviewed against `08_design_direction.md`; fix anything ugly.
