# Weapon and artifact levels

Each of the 22 campaign weapons and 6 artifacts has one level where it is the natural answer. That level's first hint
(shown on the Loadout screen and once in battle) is a `Tip:` line. Where the weapon may not be owned yet (chest or boss
drops, later weapons on levels with a fixed loadout), the level's fixed first-try loadout includes it in slot 1. After a
level is cleared the player brings their own arsenal, as before.

and 24 keep their boss hint first and feature no weapon (level 8's hint only said "Defeat the Matron", so it carries
the Cleaver tip).
