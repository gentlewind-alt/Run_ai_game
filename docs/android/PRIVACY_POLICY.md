# Privacy Policy for Monster Smash: Siege Slingshot

**Last updated: 31 October 2026**

Monster Smash: Siege Slingshot ("the game", "we") is a physics puzzle game for Android, made by Samarth Rawat. This page explains what information the game uses and why. We keep it to the minimum the game needs.

**Who it is for.** The game is meant for players aged 10 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 10. If you believe a child under 10 has used the game, contact us and we will delete any data linked to that device.

## What we do NOT collect

- Your real name, email address, phone number, address or contact list
- Your precise location
- Photos, microphone or camera data
- Payment details (the game has no real-money purchases)

## What the game stores

### On your device
Your progress, coins, settings and levels you build are saved on your phone. This stays on the device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

### Online features (Firebase, by Google)
If you use the online features (leaderboards, challenges, sharing levels), the game signs you in **anonymously**. No account, email or password is needed. We store:
- A random anonymous ID created for your install
- A display name made up by the game, such as "Brave Otter 123" (you can re-roll it, and you cannot type your own)
- Your scores and the levels you choose to share, and the "report" flags players put on shared levels

This is held in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication). It is shown to other players only as the generated name, never with your identity. Because there is no account link, we cannot tell who you are in real life.

### Ads (Google AdMob)
The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads may use your device's **advertising ID** and general device information (such as device model and approximate region) to show and measure ads and prevent fraud.
- In the EEA, UK and other regions where required, the game shows Google's consent message first. If you do not agree, you will not get personalised ads.
- Where consent is unavailable, the game requests **non-personalised ads** only.
- You can reset or turn off your advertising ID in your phone: Settings → Google → Ads.

Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and AdMob's data use: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

## How we use information

Only to run the game: save your progress, show leaderboards and shared levels, keep the community safe (reports and removing abusive content), and show ads that fund the game. We do not sell your data and we do not use it to build profiles of you.

## Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the game: Google (Firebase and AdMob). We do not sell data to anyone.

## Keeping and deleting data

Local data is deleted when you uninstall the game. For online data (anonymous ID, generated name, scores, shared levels), contact us with your generated name and we will delete it. Data tied to an anonymous ID that no longer exists on any device may also be removed in routine clean-ups.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. No method is perfectly secure, but we only keep what the game needs.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use of the game means you accept the updated policy.
