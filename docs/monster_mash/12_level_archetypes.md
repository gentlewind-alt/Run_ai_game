# Alternate level concepts for realms 4–6

These are alternative layouts for levels 13–24, using the six deception types in Stephenson’s Chapter 8. They adapt the thesis’s puzzle vocabulary to Monster Mash’s existing terrain, blocks, monsters and weapons. The sketches are schematic, not build coordinates or copies of the pictured levels.

**Legend:** `@` sling; `W` wood; `S` stone; `I` iron; `C` crystal; `m` ground monster; `F` flyer; `B` Goblin Bomber; `o` heavy stone block; `_ / \` ground or ramp; `---` beam or shelf. Distances and block sizes are not to scale.

All twelve concepts are buildable with current content: terrain polylines; wood, stone, iron and crystal blocks; the existing realm monsters and roster; and the listed registered weapons. Where a listed weapon is not in a level’s fixed loadout, it can be assigned from the existing six-shot loadout. **No new content is required.** Each 4–5-shot estimate is a design target within its current band; levels 17 and 21 allow 2–5 shots, and the rest allow 3–5. These are not solver results.

## Realm 4 · Frost Peaks

### 13 · The Cold Seam

- **Archetype:** Material analysis — attack the right material.
- **Visual reference:** p102(a), p060 (mixed materials and structures at different heights).
- **Layout:** A low crystal-and-stone shelter sits on the near shelf; a separate, higher stone spire holds an archer. An ice golem stands inside the low structure, beside the crystal strut carrying its roof.

  ```text
  @  ──>   [S C S]             [S] F
  ________/ C m C \____gap_____[C]|S|____
  ```

- **Why a greedy first shot fails:** A direct shot at the visible golem lands on the stone face. It barely shifts the roof and leaves the target protected.
- **Solve with:** Ashwood Staff’s radiant shot against the crystal support, which is weak to radiant damage; the roof then drops into the shelter.
- **Expected shots:** 4–5.
- **Buildable now:** Yes. **New content:** None.

### 14 · The Hollow Stair

- **Archetype:** Non-greedy first shot.
- **Visual reference:** p025 (terrain-built caves and separated structures), p107 (level 14).
- **Layout:** A stepped ridge makes a low tunnel with a stone lintel. A small wood brace at the far mouth carries a shelf above the tunnel; a frost wolf and hexer wait in the pocket, while an archer watches from a detached upper ledge.

  ```text
  @ ──>       upper ledge [S] F
  _______/‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾\____[S]____
           [S] m [W]     tunnel
  ```

- **Why a greedy first shot fails:** The visible wolf draws a straight shot into the lintel. The shot spends itself on the cave face and leaves the brace intact.
- **Solve with:** Iron Cleaver’s Crater ability at the far wood brace. Dropping the shelf opens the pocket for later shots.
- **Expected shots:** 4–5.
- **Buildable now:** Yes. **New content:** None.

### 15 · The One-Post Choir

- **Archetype:** Non-fixed tap time.
- **Visual reference:** p102(c), p107 (level 15; long beam balanced on one post).
- **Layout:** Two archers occupy opposite ends of a long stone-and-wood beam balanced on a single narrow wood post. A hexer sits on a lower crystal shelf, so the beam’s fall changes the next shot’s route.

  ```text
  @ ──>       F [S──W──S] F
  ___________________|W|___________
                 [C] m [C]
  ```

- **Why a greedy first shot fails:** Aiming straight at the nearest archer hits the beam and leaves its single support standing.
- **Solve with:** Hunters Boomerang’s Recall ability. Let it pass the post, then recall so its return cuts the support from the opposite side.
- **Expected shots:** 4–5.
- **Buildable now:** Yes. **New content:** None.

### 16 · The Wolf’s Cairn

- **Archetype:** Rolling or falling objects.
- **Visual reference:** p102(d), p107 (level 22; heavy object above a target).
- **Layout:** A heavy stone block rests on a short sloped shelf above the Frost Giant’s bay. A small wood stop keeps it in place; a separate high crystal-and-stone post carries an archer platform.

  ```text
  @ ──>   [C]|S| F
  _________|___|___________/‾‾\____
                         o → [S] Giant
  ```

- **Why a greedy first shot fails:** A direct hit on the Giant only deals damage; the stone block stays parked above the bay.
- **Solve with:** Hunters Pike’s Pierce ability through the wood stop. The released stone block rolls down the short ramp into the boss bay.
- **Expected shots:** 4–5.
- **Buildable now:** Yes. **New content:** None.

## Realm 5 · Ember Forge

### 17 · The Ember Relay

- **Archetype:** TNT-like chain reaction.
- **Visual reference:** p102(e), p107 (levels 25 and 27; stacked TNT beside structures).
- **Layout:** Two small wood-and-stone satellites sit at different heights, each holding a Goblin Bomber. A third bomber stands by the main forge shed; lava imps occupy the ground and a detached archer perch.

  ```text
  @ ──> [W B]       [S B] F
  _______[W]______/‾‾[S]________
                 [W B] m  m
  ```

- **Why a greedy first shot fails:** The closest ogre is outside the bomber line. Hitting it leaves the three bombers separated by their supports.
- **Solve with:** Blast Tube’s Nuke ability beside the first bomber. The blast starts the existing bomber chain across the satellites and into the shed.
- **Expected shots:** 4–5.
- **Buildable now:** Yes. **New content:** None; the chain uses existing Goblin Bombers, not explosive crates.

### 18 · The Covered Crossing

- **Archetype:** Clearing a path.
- **Visual reference:** p102(f), p025 (tunnel and terrain as cover), p107 (level 30).
- **Layout:** A low tunnel runs through a stone ridge, with a wood gate and two wood braces at its mouth. A lava imp waits at the back; two runners approach along the lower crossing, while a separate stone shelf holds a hexer.

  ```text
  @ ──>   [S] F
  _______/‾‾‾‾‾\___________
         W|W   m       → →
  ```

- **Why a greedy first shot fails:** A lob at the imp hits the gate. The tunnel remains closed, and the runners keep moving through the lower lane.
- **Solve with:** Hunters Pike’s Pierce ability through the wooden gate and braces; a later shot can travel through the opened tunnel.
- **Expected shots:** 4–5.
- **Buildable now:** Yes. **New content:** None.

### 19 · The Iron Hinge

- **Archetype:** Material analysis — choose the weak anchor.
- **Visual reference:** p102(a), p060 (material variety across separate structures).
- **Layout:** A stone forge stack sits on a narrow iron hinge over a low shed. A small crystal cap carries a lava imp; a second satellite has an ogre behind a wood screen.

  ```text
  @ ──> [W] m        [C] m
  _______[W]_____[S][I][S]____/‾\___
                    [S] m
  ```

- **Why a greedy first shot fails:** The broad stone face absorbs a frontal hit while the iron hinge continues to hold the stack above the shed.
- **Solve with:** Black Hole Marble’s void shot and Crush ability at the iron hinge, which is weak to void; the stack folds down and exposes the monsters below.
- **Expected shots:** 4–5.
- **Buildable now:** Yes. **New content:** None.

### 20 · The Empty Throne

- **Archetype:** Non-greedy first shot.
- **Visual reference:** p102(b), p025 (multiple structures at different distances), p107 (level 10).
- **Layout:** The Forge Tyrant is visible behind the main stone frame. A narrow wood satellite brace holds a long overhead slab whose fall will open the boss bay; a high ledge carries two lava imps.

  ```text
  @ ──> [W]             F   F
  _______[S]______[S]═══[S]____
                 [S] Tyrant
  ```

- **Why a greedy first shot fails:** A direct shot at the Tyrant hits the front frame, leaving the overhead slab and its narrow satellite brace untouched.
- **Solve with:** Hunters Pike’s Pierce ability through the small wood brace. The slab falls away from the entrance and opens a clean follow-up line to the boss.
- **Expected shots:** 4–5.
- **Buildable now:** Yes. **New content:** None.

## Realm 6 · Sky Citadel

### 21 · The High-Wind Window

- **Archetype:** Non-fixed tap time.
- **Visual reference:** p102(c), p060 (islands and structures at varied heights), p107 (level 22).
- **Layout:** A low stone dock, a floating crystal shelf with a storm mage, and a distant iron spire with two harpies form three separate targets. The small opening between shelves is the useful burst window.

  ```text
  @ ──>             [C] m        [I] F
  _______[S]_________‾‾‾‾_________[I] F___
  ```

- **Why a greedy first shot fails:** Bursting too early scatters bombs below the crystal shelf; too late sends them beyond the iron spire.
- **Solve with:** Pinata MIRV’s Burst ability, tapped as the projectile crosses the gap between the floating shelf and spire so the bombs fan across both heights.
- **Expected shots:** 4–5.
- **Buildable now:** Yes. **New content:** None.

### 22 · The Hanging Weather Vane

- **Archetype:** Rolling or falling objects.
- **Visual reference:** p102(d), p107 (levels 19, 20 and 22; a heavy object above the target).
- **Layout:** A long stone slab balances on one narrow wood post above a ground-level ogre and storm mage. A heavy stone block sits on the slab’s short tilted end; two harpies circle a separate high shelf.

  ```text
  @ ──>        [C] F          F
  _____________[S]────o___________
                     |W|
                  m  m   [S]_____
  ```

- **Why a greedy first shot fails:** Firing at the ground monsters leaves the iron post intact; the slab and stone weight stay suspended.
- **Solve with:** Hunters Pike’s Pierce ability through the wood post. The post gives way and the slab and stone block fall through the ground cluster.
- **Expected shots:** 4–5.
- **Buildable now:** Yes. **New content:** None.

### 23 · The Storm Relay

- **Archetype:** TNT-like chain reaction.
- **Visual reference:** p102(e), p107 (levels 25 and 27; chain setup across structures).
- **Layout:** Three small stone-and-wood platforms step upward toward the main hall. Existing Goblin Bombers stand on the first and third platforms, with a bomber beside a low shed; storm mages occupy a detached crystal shelf and harpies fly above.

  ```text
  @ ──> [W B]       [S B] F
  _______[W]______/‾‾[S]_____[C] m
                [W B]       F
  ```

- **Why a greedy first shot fails:** A direct shot at the nearest mage is blocked by the first platform and leaves the bomber chain out of reach.
- **Solve with:** Steersong Arrow’s Arm ability. Set its explosive tip beside the first bomber to trigger the existing chain; split only if the upper platform needs a second impact.
- **Expected shots:** 4–5.
- **Buildable now:** Yes. **New content:** None; no TNT crate is needed.

### 24 · The Eye of the Citadel

- **Archetype:** Clearing a path.
- **Visual reference:** p102(f), p025 (cave and overhead terrain), p107 (levels 29 and 30; protected target behind a tunnel mouth).
- **Layout:** The Storm Sovereign waits in a low stone pocket behind a wood grille held by iron uprights and a short crystal roof. A separate wood shed protects an archer, while two harpies occupy a floating shelf beyond the entrance.

  ```text
  @ ──> [W] F          [C] F
  _______[W]______/‾‾‾‾‾‾\____
                 [S][W][S] m
  ```

- **Why a greedy first shot fails:** The boss is visible through the grille, but a straight shot spends itself on the wood slats and does not open a route into the pocket.
- **Solve with:** Hunters Pike’s Pierce ability through the wood grille. The iron uprights remain as a frame while the slats break away, opening the tunnel for the next shot.
- **Expected shots:** 4–5.
- **Buildable now:** Yes. **New content:** None.

## Design notes

- The layouts use the thesis’s Chapter 8 deception types as a design vocabulary: material choice, a deliberately indirect opening move, ability timing, unstable objects, chained explosions, and staged path clearing.
- The thesis’s Chapter 2 and Chapter 4 emphasis on stable structures and varied, solvable levels translates here to supports that hold at spawn, varied materials and dimensions, and a readable route to each target. Those properties still need to be checked in the actual level and game physics.
- The sketches borrow visual cues from the cited pages: separated structures, shelves, ramps, caves, balanced beams, falling weights, dispersed monsters and flyers. They are new layouts; no thesis level is copied.
- “Expected shots” means an authoring target only. The solver can assess its own shot policy and the configured band; it cannot prove that a level has no other solution or that a human will read the intended puzzle. Run the level sweep and a human playtest after any concept is built.
